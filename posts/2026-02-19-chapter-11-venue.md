Venue reform arguments usually get framed as a fairness question. The more useful
framing is a **speed** question.

Judges who run fast dockets produce different plans than judges who don't — not
because they rule differently on the law, but because a compressed timeline
changes who has leverage. Liquidity runs out on a clock. Every week of delay is a
transfer from the fulcrum to whoever is funding the case.

## The mechanism

1. Case filed with 13 weeks of runway.
2. DIP milestones set at 90 days.
3. Any contested valuation fight takes longer than that.
4. Therefore the valuation fight never happens.

Step 4 is the whole ballgame, and it's determined mostly by the calendar.
