The stated capital structure is a starting hypothesis, not a fact. Before you can
locate the fulcrum you have to answer three questions the cap table won't.

## 1. Where does the EBITDA actually sit?

Guarantor vs. non-guarantor splits are the single largest source of surprise in
distressed analysis. If 40% of EBITDA sits at non-guarantors, the "secured" debt
is only secured against 60% of the business.

## 2. What left the box?

Unrestricted subsidiary designations, IP transfers, and sale-leasebacks. Trace
every asset that moved in the 24 months before filing.

## 3. Who can actually block?

A 33% blocking position in the fulcrum class is worth more than a 60% position
one class up. Count the votes, not the dollars.

---

Only after those three does the waterfall mean anything.
