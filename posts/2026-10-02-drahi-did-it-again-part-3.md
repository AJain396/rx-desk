Patrick Drahi's deals tend to  a lot of debt. That’s his “house special”. So when Optimum ended up here, it really couldn’t be much of a surprise to anyone who'd followed him.

To an extent, this was predictable. We see four drivers:

- Leverage from day one: Altice paid $34.90 a share in cash for Cablevision in 2016, funded with $8.6 billion of new debt at CSC Holdings and $3.3 billion of cash from Altice and co-investors. Another $5.9 billion of existing debt stayed in place.
- The track record: in February 2025, Altice France agreed with creditors to cut its debt from about €24.8 billion to about €16.9 billion, and creditors took 45% of the equity. The deal closed on October 1, 2025.
- Rates: the Fed started hiking in March 2022 and got to 5.25–5.50% by July 2023.
- The business: consolidated adjusted EBITDA went from about $4.4 billion in 2021 to about $3.4 billion in 2025.

On timing, we'd start with rates. Most of Optimum's bonds pay a fixed coupon that was set years ago, so the Fed going from near zero to 5.25–5.50% didn't change those payments. It starts to matter once the debt comes due and has to be refinanced. For example, CSC still has 5.5% notes due in April 2027, while the newest money Optimum has raised (the UnSub loans) costs 9%.

The Altice France deal is another piece worth looking at. As part of it, Altice France agreed to put previously unrestricted assets back under its lenders' collateral. Optimum took a lesson from that: we saw it moved Optimum East out in June 2026 before its lenders could ask.

However, the EBITDA line is what’s really worrying. A growing cable business could probably get refinanced without much drama. This one has lost roughly $1 billion of EBITDA since 2021. It was set up to carry debt assuming cash flows would stay steady. They didn’t.

It’s a pattern. Debt gets loaded on, negotiating happens years later, and assets get moved around. So to an extent, this was predictable. Just the timing of rolling what was cheap debt ruined the plan.

## Deal Parties

- Borrower: Optimum Communications, Inc. (f/k/a Altice USA) / CSC Holdings, LLC; unrestricted subsidiary CSC Investments II LLC. No bankruptcy filing.
- Financial Advisors / Bankers: Company: Evercore (since September 2025; Moelis before that). Lenders: PJT Partners (last confirmed April 2025).
- Major Creditors / Creditor Groups: co-op including Apollo, Ares, BlackRock and Oaktree (named in Optimum's lawsuit).
- Law Firms: Company: Kirkland & Ellis (hired September 2025, not reconfirmed since); White & Case and Quinn Emanuel on the June 2026 transactions. Lenders: Akin Gump (last confirmed April 2025).
